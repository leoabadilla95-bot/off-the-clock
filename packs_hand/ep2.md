# Episode 2 production pack: "Bullseye"

Everything needed to make the 95-second second episode, shot by shot: 22 shots cut into 29 pieces. It opens on the cliffhanger, Priya's "Mr. Co—", then cuts to Friday night at Bullseye Axe House, where Hunter finally lets go and Jo starts to recognize him. Work top to bottom: lock the references, generate every keyframe and get it approved, then animate, record voices, and assemble. Wherever a prompt says [HUNTER], [ABBY], [PRIYA], [JO] or [STAFFER], paste that character's locked descriptor in full (Step 1). Never paraphrase it.

## Step 5: Assembly and checks

Cut in CapCut or DaVinci Resolve on a 1080×1920 timeline. The 22 shots are cut into 29 pieces that add up to exactly 95 seconds.

1. **Lay picture.** Trim each cut to its length, in order (1, 2, 3a, 3b, 4–7, 8a–8c, 9, 10, 11a, 11b, 12–15, 16a–16c, 17, 18a, 18b, 19–22): 3 · 5 · 4 · 3 · 3 · 3 · 3 · 4 · 5 · 4 · 4 · 2 · 4 · 4 · 2 · 2 · 4 · 3 · 2 · 4 · 4 · 4 · 2 · 5 · 2 · 2 · 2 · 2 · 4.
2. **Composite.** Screen A onto the scanner (shot 5), screen B onto Abby's phone (shot 17), screen C onto Jo's phone with the pinch-zoom (shot 20).
3. **Lip-sync and place lines.** Sync lines 1–24 from Step 3. Cut Priya hard on "Co" in shot 1 and keep the staffer's pause before "Bro."
4. **Grade.** Shots 1–3 keep the cool steel-blue "Glass" look. From the whip in shot 4 everything is warm: Edison bulbs, tungsten, red brick. Shots 19–22 stay warm in color but get a touch more contrast so the end feels tense.
5. **Music and sound.**
   - Office room tone only under shots 1–3, with one muted pluck in shot 2.
   - Warm music bed starts on the whip into shot 4.
   - The deal "ding" in shot 5, the same sound as Episode 1.
   - Comic stings on each clatter in shot 10.
   - All sound falls away in shot 12; the THUNK and a full swell in shot 13.
   - Shutter click in shot 17; music softens under 16c.
   - Music cuts to 0.5s of silence in shot 21 and stays out through the end.
6. **Text and captions.** *Friday. West Loop.* in the top third of shot 4, word-by-word captions above the bottom 20%, and the end card *Episode 2 of 10*.
7. **Export.** H.264, 1080×1920, 30 fps.
