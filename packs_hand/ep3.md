# Episode 3 production pack: "Masa"

Everything needed to make the 120-second third episode, shot by shot: 25 shots cut into 35 pieces. It picks up on Jo's "Like, right now" at the axe bar, cuts to Abby's Pilsen apartment and Hunter's empty penthouse, then spends Saturday at Doña Lupe's tamale class, where Hunter makes something with his hands for the first time and a fraud call on speaker gives away his real name. Work top to bottom: lock the references, generate every keyframe and get it approved, then animate, record voices, and assemble. Wherever a prompt says [HUNTER], [ABBY], [JO] or [LUPE], paste that character's locked descriptor in full (Step 1). Never paraphrase it.
