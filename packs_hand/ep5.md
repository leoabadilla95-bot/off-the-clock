## Step 5: Assembly and checks

Cut in CapCut or DaVinci Resolve on a 1080×1920 timeline. The 25 shots are cut into 31 pieces that add up to exactly 97 seconds.

1. **Lay picture.** Place the shots in order and trim each cut to its length, in order (1–4, 5a, 5b, 6–8, 9a, 9b, 10–14, 15a, 15b, 16, 17, 18a, 18b, 19, 20a, 20b, 20c, 21–25): 2 · 4 · 2 · 4 · 3 · 4 · 3 · 2 · 2 · 2 · 4 · 2 · 2 · 4 · 3 · 2 · 9 · 4 · 4 · 2 · 3 · 2 · 2 · 3 · 4 · 3 · 5 · 3 · 2 · 2 · 4.
2. **Composite.** Add screen A in shot 8, screen B in shot 23 and screen C in shots 24 and 25, plus the *Saturday. Wicker Park.* text in shot 6. Cut the 1s watch twist into 5a under "Spreadsheet thing."
3. **Lip-sync and place lines.** Sync lines 1–23 from Step 3. Leave real pauses before "…This is pretty close." and "…No."
4. **Grade.** The office is the cool "Glass" look. Drift goes blue-violet, then the float room is the darkest, stillest blue in the series. The sauna is the warmest amber of the episode, and the locker room at the end returns to cold blue-violet: the warmth should feel taken away.
5. **Music and sound.**
   - Tension drone under shots 1–4, gone when Grant leaves.
   - Ambient hum from the match cut (shot 6) through the lockers.
   - Total silence in the float room (shots 11–13): no music, only breath and water.
   - Soft piano returns with the steam hiss in shot 14, lifts on the laugh in shot 19, and drops out under Abby's line in shot 21.
   - A single sub-bass heartbeat in shot 23, a buzz in shot 24, then silence to the freeze.
6. **Text and captions.** *Saturday. Wicker Park.* in the top third of shot 6. Burn in word-by-word captions above the bottom 20%, and add the end card *Episode 5 of 10*.
7. **Export.** H.264, 1080×1920, 30 fps.
