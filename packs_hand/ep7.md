## Step 5: Assembly and checks

Cut in CapCut or DaVinci Resolve on a 1080×1920 timeline. The 23 shots are cut into 31 pieces that add up to exactly 94 seconds.

2. **Composite.** Screen A on Grant's tablet in shot 10, screen B on the document in shot 20.
3. **Lip-sync and place lines.** Sync lines 1–26 from Step 3. Leave a beat before "…That's my name" and before Hunter's "…Yeah."
4. **Grade.** The dock starts in full gold and drains toward blue by shot 6; that change is the scene. The boardroom and CEO office are the coldest, darkest looks in the series: black glass, steel blue, no warmth except the desk lamp. The 4th floor keeps the cool "Glass" look, with Abby's mustard beanie as the only warm note.
5. **Music and sound.**
   - Dock: no music; yacht party muffled behind, water against the pilings.
   - A cold drone under the boardroom (shots 7–11); a heartbeat of sub-bass on the list (shot 10).
   - Light plucky music under the team rally (shots 12–15), stopping dead on the elevator chime (shot 16).
   - Silence in the office (shot 19), then a rising tone under the pen (shot 22) that cuts on the freeze.
   - Office room tone under the 4th floor.
6. **Text and captions.** *Monday. 22nd floor.* in the top third, word-by-word captions above the bottom 20%, end card *Episode 7 of 10*.
7. **Export.** H.264, 1080×1920, 30 fps.
