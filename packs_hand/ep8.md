# Episode 8 production pack: "Yes, And"

Everything needed to make the 110-second improv episode, shot by shot: 26 shots cut into 30 pieces. Work top to bottom: lock the references, generate every keyframe and get it approved, then animate, record voices, and assemble. Wherever a prompt says [HUNTER], [ABBY], [PRIYA], [TEAM], [INSTRUCTOR] or [HOST], paste that locked descriptor in full (Step 1). Never paraphrase it. The episode runs cold office, then warm theater, then warm bar that goes silent: keep that color script.

## Step 5: Assembly and checks

Cut in CapCut or DaVinci Resolve on a 1080×1920 timeline. The 26 shots are cut into 30 pieces that add up to exactly 110 seconds.

1. **Lay picture.** Place the shots in order and trim each cut to its length (1–7, 8a, 8b, 9–14, 15a–15c, 16, 17, 18a, 18b, 19–26): 3 · 2 · 4 · 3 · 5 · 4 · 5 · 5 · 4 · 2 · 3 · 4 · 8 · 3 · 5 · 4 · 6 · 4 · 2 · 4 · 5 · 4 · 3 · 2 · 2 · 2 · 2 · 2 · 3 · 5.
2. **Composite.** Screen A on the phone in cut 5, screen D on the paper in cuts 1–2, screens B and C on the deck in cuts 22–23, and the title card on cut 4.
3. **Lip-sync and place lines.** Sync lines 1–18 from Step 3. Leave a real pause after "I think…" before the off-screen line in 15b.
4. **Grade.** Cuts 1–3 get the cold "Glass" look. The theater and bar are warm tungsten. From cut 22, slowly drain warmth and saturation so that by cut 26 the bar feels as cold as the office.
5. **Music and sound.**
   - No music in the office; just the pen click.
   - Bouncy music kicks in on the marquee (cut 4) and drops out when they hit the stage (cut 10).
   - The deal "ding" on the voucher scan in cut 5 only.
   - Laughter big in cut 13, the "Awww" in 15c, then 0.5s of near silence under cut 16.
   - Music back on "And scene!" through the toast; bar noise fades from cut 22, heartbeat sub-bass in cut 23, total silence in cut 24.
6. **Text and captions.** Thursday. Old Town. in the top third of cut 4, word-by-word captions above the bottom 20%, and the end card Episode 8 of 10.
7. **Export.** H.264, 1080×1920, 30 fps.
