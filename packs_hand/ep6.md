## Step 5: Assembly and checks

Cut in CapCut or DaVinci Resolve on a 1080×1920 timeline. The 26 shots are cut into 34 pieces that add up to exactly 105 seconds.

1. **Lay picture.** Place the shots in order and trim each cut to its length (1–14, 15a–15d, 16, 17a–17d, 18a–18c, 19–26): 3 · 5 · 5 · 2 · 3 · 2 · 2 · 3 · 2 · 3 · 4 · 2 · 2 · 2 · 5 · 5 · 8 · 2 · 2 · 3 · 4 · 4 · 2 · 3 · 2 · 4 · 2 · 2 · 2 · 3 · 3 · 2 · 2 · 5.
2. **Composite.** Screen A in shot 1, the deck in shot 5, screens C–F on the phone in shots 7–10.
3. **Lip-sync and place lines.** Sync lines 1–22 from Step 3. Leave a real beat before "I want it to be mine" and before "Does your board know?"
4. **Grade.** Locker room stays blue-violet with the one warm downlight. The penthouse is the coldest image in the season so far: steel blue, no warmth except the phone glow on his face in shot 9. The lake is full golden hour and should feel like a release. The yacht's arrival keeps the gold but adds hard contrast.
5. **Music and sound.**
   - Locker room: room tone and distant water only; the watch buckle (shot 4) is the loudest thing in the scene.
   - Penthouse: HVAC hum, slow taps, the shimmer on the deal and the deal "ding" in shot 9 (his first; same sound as Episode 1).
   - Warm acoustic guitar comes up on the dock (shot 11), rides the splash war, settles to a pad under the Lisbon talk, then one piano note in shot 19.
   - The yacht horn (shot 22) and bass-heavy party music bury the piano, then cut dead when Celeste lowers her sunglasses (shot 25).
   - Water lapping under everything on the lake.
6. **Text and captions.** *2:14 AM* and *Sunday. Lake Michigan.* in the top third, word-by-word captions above the bottom 20%, end card *Episode 6 of 10*.
7. **Export.** H.264, 1080×1920, 30 fps.
