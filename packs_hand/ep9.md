3. **Lip-sync and place lines.** Sync lines 1–31 from Step 3. Reuse Episode 8's "…Mr. Cole?" take for line 1. Leave a beat before 10c, before 18c, and between "All-hands." and "Camera on."
4. **Grade.** The bar starts at Episode 8's drained warmth and stays there. Office and CEO office get the cold "Glass" look. The apartment is warm string light. The studio is the warmest grade in the series: golden sun, dust, rain on glass.
5. **Music and sound.**

   - Phone buzz in cut 11, chair scrape in cut 12.
   - Rain and the wheel hum under the studio; the door chime in cut 16.
