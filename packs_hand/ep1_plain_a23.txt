
# Episode 1 production pack: "Hunter from Ops"
Everything needed to make the 80-second pitch episode, shot by shot. Work top to bottom: lock the references, generate every keyframe and get it approved, then animate, record voices, and assemble. Wherever a prompt says [HUNTER], [ABBY] or [PRIYA], paste that character's locked descriptor in full (Step 1). Never paraphrase it.

## Step 2: Shot by shot
For each shot: generate the keyframe (a still, using the character reference images), get it approved, then animate it with the video prompt for 5–8 seconds and trim to the length shown. Lines are recorded separately in ElevenLabs (Step 3) and lip-synced. All shots are vertical 9:16.
### Shot 1 · 0:00 · ECU · 3s · Lobby

Keyframe: Extreme close-up of a man's left hand and wrist, his thumb resting on a plain steel wristwatch, navy quarter-zip cuff, cool blue morning light from a glass atrium behind, shallow depth of field, 85mm lens, vertical 9:16, photorealistic.

Video (Kling): The thumb slowly twists the watch a quarter turn and back, a nervous habit. Static camera. Subtle breathing movement in the wrist.

Audio: low ambient drone.

Post: on-screen text in the top third: He owns the building.
### Shot 2 · 0:03 · WS, locked-off · 3s · Lobby

Keyframe: Wide shot, [HUNTER] walking alone through a double-height glass lobby with a moss wall, small in frame, head down, passing a large framed photo that is blurry and taken from behind. Perfectly symmetrical composition, cool daylight, vertical 9:16, cinematic. (Use the lobby plate as a reference image.)

Video (Veo 3.1): He walks steadily past the framed photo without looking at it, eyes on the floor. Locked-off tripod camera.

Audio: footsteps echoing on concrete, drone continues.

Post: text: Nobody here knows his face.
### Shot 3 · 0:06 · INS · 2s · Lobby

Keyframe: the badge-reader plate.

Video (Runway): A hand taps a white ID badge against the reader; the light flips from red to green.

Audio: soft beep.

Post: composite the reader's screen text: HUNTER C — OPS (TEMP).
### Shot 4 · 0:08 · MS · 2s · 4th floor

Keyframe: Medium shot, [HUNTER] sitting at a bare desk in an open-plan tech office, half-unwrapped protein bar in hand, fluorescent light, cool palette. An empty office chair at the right edge of frame. Vertical 9:16. (Reference: the 4th-floor plate.)

Video (Kling): An office chair rolls into frame from the right and bumps his desk. He looks up, startled, still holding the bar. Static camera.

Audio: chair-wheel rattle, a soft thunk. Playful plucked strings start on the bump.
### Shot 5 · 0:10 · MCU + cutaway · 7s (5a 5s on Abby, 5b 2s on Hunter) · 4th floor

Keyframe: Medium close-up of [ABBY] leaning back in a rolled-over office chair, holding a mustard-yellow mug, squinting with amused suspicion, looking slightly screen-right, eye level, 50mm, open-plan office softly blurred behind, vertical 9:16.

Video (Veo 3.1, with Abby's hero, 3/4 and wardrobe images as ingredients): She speaks to someone just off camera, eyebrows up, teasing, small head tilt at the end. Static camera.

Line (ABBY): "Okay, I have to ask. Is that the same protein bar? Like, same flavor, three days?"
### Shot 6 · 0:17 · OTS + reverse · 6s (6a 2s on Hunter, 6b 4s on Abby) · 4th floor

Keyframe: Over-the-shoulder shot from behind [ABBY] (the back of her mustard beanie and hoop earring soft in the foreground), focused on [HUNTER] mid-chew, slightly defensive, looking screen-left, vertical 9:16.

## Step 5: Assembly and checks
Cut in CapCut or DaVinci Resolve on a 1080×1920 timeline. The 17 shots are cut into 25 pieces that add up to exactly 80 seconds.

Lay picture. Place the shots in order and trim each cut to its length, in order (1, 2, 3, 4, 5a, 5b, 6a, 6b, 7, 8a–8d, 9a, 9b, 10, 11, 12a–12c, 13–17): 3 · 3 · 2 · 2 · 5 · 2 · 2 · 4 · 3 · 3 · 4 · 4 · 2 · 5 · 2 · 2 · 2 · 3 · 4 · 3 · 2 · 9 · 3 · 2 · 4.

Composite. Add screens A–D onto the green phone screens, plus the badge text in shot 3.

Lip-sync and place lines. Sync lines 1–18 from Step 3. Leave a beat of silence before "Define 'something'" and before Priya's line.

Grade. Lobby and office get the cool "Glass" look. Shot 14's golden light is the one warm moment, and it should feel like a reward.

Music and sound.

Drone under shots 1–3.

Plucked strings start on the chair bump (shot 4), drop out under the question (shot 9), return warmer in shot 14, and stop dead at the elevator (shot 15).

Use the deal "ding" in shot 13 only.

Add office room tone under everything.

Text and captions. Put the two opening lines in the top third (shots 1–2), burn in word-by-word captions above the bottom 20%, and add the end card Episode 1 of 10.

Export. H.264, 1080×1920, 30 fps.
### Final checks before Groupon sees it

Hunter, Abby and Priya match their reference sheets in every shot

Hunter wears the navy quarter-zip and the watch in every shot he's in

Abby's beanie, hoops and claw clip are there in every shot she's in

No garbled text, real logos or extra fingers anywhere

Every phone screen is legible for at least 1.5 seconds

The captions match the audio word for word
