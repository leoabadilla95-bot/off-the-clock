## Episode 10: Off the Clock

**Stakes in one line:** Grant has the board on Monday's all-hands, a signature request and photos of Hunter with Abby, so Hunter has one call to tell two thousand people who he is.

**Story in brief.** Beginning: Grant opens the all-hands to a black "camera off" tile and starts on the restructure, and the borrowed desk on four is empty. Then the tile flickers on, and Hunter is sitting at that desk. Middle: he tells the company who he is and that he lied, cancels the layoffs, and turns Grant's blackmail text over to the board, so Grant leaves the call and the company. He launches "Off the Clock", a monthly Groupon credit for every employee, and four coworkers find four very different deals. Abby's promotion comes through from the head of design, with Hunter recused, and his watch goes in a drawer. End: Sunday at Maxwell Street, a twelve-dollar sweater, a new sticky note, a Lisbon jar at sixty percent, and the kiss they almost had on the lake.
