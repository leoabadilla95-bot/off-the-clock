## Episode 5: Unplugged

**Stakes in one line:** Grant has found Hunter on the 4th floor and knows he's lying, and in a cedar sauna Abby tells Hunter the one story that makes the list in his bag unforgivable.

**Story in brief.** Beginning: Grant corners "Hunter from Ops" at Abby's desk; Hunter whispers "culture audit", and Grant doesn't believe a word. Middle: at a Wicker Park float spa the phones and the watch go in a locker, and in the sauna, after the banter runs out, Abby tells him her dad was laid off when she was twelve, in a fifteen-minute meeting, by a man who never knew his name. End: Hunter almost confesses, then lies instead. Back at the locker, fourteen missed calls: Grant has moved the board vote to tomorrow, and Abby is reading over his shoulder.
