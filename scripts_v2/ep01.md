## Episode 1: Hunter from Ops

**Stakes in one line:** Hunter has two weeks to decide whether to cut 200 jobs, starting with the 4th-floor design team, and the woman who adopts him there is up for a promotion on Friday.

**Story in brief.** Beginning: the board hands Hunter a layoff plan, and he goes undercover on the floor that's first on the list. Middle: Abby clocks the new guy (nice watch, sad lunch) and, between her own nerves about Friday, talks him into a cheap Friday-night date. End: one date, he promises himself, then the truth. His assistant arrives on the floor and nearly says his name.
