## Episode 4: Camera Off

**Stakes in one line:** Abby has started counting Hunter's lies, Grant goes over Hunter's head and moves Design's "efficiency review" to this Friday, and the CEO login Hunter hides behind leads Grant straight to the 4th floor.

**Story in brief.** Beginning: "Mr. Cole" becomes "a family name, it's complicated, it's my dad's card", and Abby starts a count. Middle: at Monday's all-hands the CEO "joins remotely, camera off": it's Hunter, crammed in a supply closet, typing. He calls on the 4th floor, and Abby tells the black square exactly what the company is missing without knowing it's him. Then Grant takes the screen and announces an efficiency review, Design first, this Friday. Abby, scared, writes three cheap dates on a sticky note and makes him pick. End: Grant traces the CEO's login to a 4th-floor closet, steps off the elevator, and sees Hunter.
