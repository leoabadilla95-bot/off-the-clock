## Episode 9: Kiln

**Stakes in one line:** Abby knows who Hunter is and that her name is on his list, the signing deadline has passed with nothing signed, and Grant is going to the board with proof that the CEO is dating someone he was about to lay off.

**Story in brief.** Beginning: Abby drops the deck and walks out of the bar. Middle: she goes dark. Jo offers to hate Hunter on her behalf, and Priya warns Hunter that Grant is telling the board he is compromised. Then Jo, of all people, texts Hunter where Abby is: the pottery class, the last date on the sticky note. End: at the wheel he tells her everything, and she tells him she always knew when he was lying. They make one hideous bowl. Then Grant's threat lights up his phone, she reads it, and she dares him to tell everyone anyway: Monday, all-hands, camera on.
