## Episode 8: Yes, And

**Stakes in one line:** The layoff list is due on Friday with Abby's name on it, and Hunter brings it in his bag to the team night out she planned to keep everyone from panicking.

**Story in brief.** Beginning: Hunter caps the pen without signing and takes the deck with him to improv. Middle: Abby's $60 Groupon gets six of them in the door, but her promotion review is suddenly cancelled, and Hunter tells her he knows nothing about it, which is lie number three. On stage, playing a first date where one person has a secret, he tells the truth as a joke. The crowd laughs. Abby doesn't. End: at the bar his bag falls, and Abby picks up a deck prepared for Hunter Cole, CEO, with her own name inside.
