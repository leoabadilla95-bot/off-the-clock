## Episode 6: Low Tide

**Stakes in one line:** Grant's emergency vote passes and the layoffs land on Hunter's desk for signature, on the same day he finally understands what Abby's money is for.

**Story in brief.** Beginning: Hunter talks his way past "board vote" with a family-business dodge Abby lets slide (barely). At 2 a.m., unable to sleep, he opens Groupon for the first time and books the next item on her sticky note himself: a $45 sunset kayak tour. Middle: on the dock, Priya's text: the vote passed, signature by Friday. On the water there's a splash war, a pan confession, and Abby's Lisbon jar; when he offers to "cover it", she says she wants the trip to be hers, and he privately decides to kill the layoffs. End: they lean in for a kiss, and a party yacht cuts across it. His ex, Celeste, steps off and says his real name.
