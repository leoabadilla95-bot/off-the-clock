## Episode 3: Masa

**Stakes in one line:** Jo thinks Hunter is Lumen's founder, Abby laughs it off and secretly isn't sure, and Hunter spends a Saturday falling for the neighborhood his list would gut, until his own bank says his real name out loud.

**Story in brief.** Beginning: the photo is too blurry to prove anything ("same ears", Jo insists); Abby jokes it away and doubts it the second nobody's looking. Saturday she finds a $34 tamale class in Groupon's Classes and texts him. Middle: at Masa Mía, Doña Lupe clocks his soft "office hands" and likes him anyway; at the steamer Abby tells him her mom fed a whole block off a twenty-dollar class, and Hunter sees the list in his bag. End: he buys aguas frescas for the room, his card flags for fraud, and the bank on speaker asks for Mr. Cole.
