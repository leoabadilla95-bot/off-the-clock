## Episode 7: The List

**Stakes in one line:** Hunter has decided to kill the layoffs, but Grant has a counter-move: refuse to sign and the board replaces him, and Abby Reyes is now on the list by name.

**Story in brief.** Beginning: on the dock, Celeste outs "Hunter Cole" and his Paris habits, and walks off delighted; Hunter tries "family name" again, and Abby quietly counts lie number two. Middle: Monday on 22, Hunter tells Grant he won't sign. Grant smiles: then the board signs, with a CEO who will. He knows about the 4th floor, and he's put Abby on the list by name. Downstairs, Abby, oblivious, rallies her scared team into a cheap improv night and saves Hunter a seat. End: night, alone in the black-glass office, Priya gone, the pen over the signature line, and a drop of ink falls.
