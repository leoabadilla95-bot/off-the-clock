## Episode 2: Bullseye

**Stakes in one line:** Grant starts asking where Hunter disappears to every day, Abby's promotion review is suddenly frozen, and the one person Abby trusts most sits down across from Hunter and starts googling.

**Story in brief.** Beginning: Priya turns "Mr. Co—" into "copier", and Abby doesn't buy it. Upstairs, Grant corners Priya about Hunter's blank calendar, and by Friday morning Abby's review has been postponed by the COO's office. Middle: at the axe bar Hunter tries to pay a prepaid voucher with a fat roll of cash, Abby quietly adds it to her list, then she fixes his stance and he hits his first bullseye and laughs like he forgot he could. End: Jo arrives from a shift, clocks the watch, googles Lumen's founder, and zooms in on a grainy photo.
